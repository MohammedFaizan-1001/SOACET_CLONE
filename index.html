SOACET – School of Aeronautics Common Entrance Test

SOACET (School of Aeronautics Common Entrance Test) is the official entrance examination conducted by the School of Aeronautics for admission into its prestigious aviation and engineering programs. SOACET is a national-level test designed to select talented students and provide them an opportunity to build a successful career in the aviation industry.

SOACET is more than just an exam – it is the gateway to Aeronautical Engineering and Aircraft Maintenance Engineering education.

What is SOACET?

SOACET is an entrance examination organised by the School of Aeronautics to offer admissions into professional aviation courses such as:

  • B.Tech Aeronautical Engineering
  • Aircraft Maintenance Engineering (AME)
  • Integrated B.Tech + AME Program
  • Other aviation-related technical courses

Through SOACET, students get a fair, transparent, and merit-based opportunity to join one of India’s most reputed aviation institutes.

Why SOACET is Important?

SOACET ensures:

  • Standardised admission process
  • Merit-based student selection
  • Scholarship opportunities
  • Academic quality and transparency
  • Right career guidance for aviation aspirants
  • It helps students begin their aviation journey on a strong and professional platform.

Who Can Apply for SOACET?

Eligibility for B.Tech Aeronautical Engineering

  • Passed or appearing 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM)
  • Minimum marks as per AICTE norms

Eligibility for AME Course

  • 10+2 with PCM
  • Or equivalent qualification as per DGCA guidelines

Both boys and girls are eligible to apply.

SOACET Exam Pattern

The SOACET exam evaluates the student’s:

  • Technical aptitude
  • Logical reasoning
  • Basic science and mathematics skills
  • General awareness

Subjects Covered

  • Physics
  • Mathematics
  • Chemistry
  • English & Reasoning
  • General Knowledge

The exam is conducted in an objective (MCQ) format to test conceptual understanding.

Mode of Examination

SOACET is conducted in:

  • Online mode
  • Offline mode at selected centres

Students can select their preferred mode while applying.

Scholarship Through SOACET

One of the biggest advantages of appearing in SOACET is the scholarship opportunity. Based on SOACET performance:

  • Merit scholarships are awarded
  • Tuition fee concessions are provided
  • Deserving students get financial support

This makes aviation education affordable and accessible for talented candidates.

How to Apply for SOACET?

Simple Online Application Process

  • Register online on the official SOACET portal
  • Fill the application form
  • Pay the registration fee
  • Download the admit card
  • Appear for the SOACET exam
  • Check result
  • Attend counselling
  • Confirm admission

Benefits of Appearing in SOACET

  • Direct admission opportunity
  • Transparent selection system
  • Scholarship benefits
  • Career counselling support
  • Priority seat allocation

SOACET opens the door to a professional and globally recognised aviation career.
